## English

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Reaction Time app ("the app") collects, how it is used and what choices you have. The app is free and supported by advertising. By using the app you agree to this policy.

### 1. Information the developer collects

The developer does **not** collect, store or have access to any personal information about you. The app has no registration, no accounts and no developer-operated servers.

### 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game results (reaction times, number of attempts, records and averages) locally on your device so it can show your statistics. This data:

- is not sent to the developer;
- may be included in your device's standard Android backup to your own Google account, if backup is enabled in your Android settings;
- can be deleted at any time with the Reset button in the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

### 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process the following data from your device:

- Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier) and other device identifiers;
- IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version);
- interactions with ads (views, clicks) and app usage data;
- diagnostic and performance data (for example, crash logs related to the ads SDK);
- on Android 13 and later, Google may also use the Android Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution reporting).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer, and is transmitted in encrypted form (HTTPS). Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Advertising technologies at Google: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- AdMob ad technology providers: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903>

### 4. Your choices

- **Personalized ads:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device). After the ID is deleted you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
- **Google ad settings:** <https://adssettings.google.com>
- **Privacy Sandbox:** on Android 13+ you can manage ad topics and ad measurement in Settings → Security & privacy → Ads privacy.
- **Local data:** delete it with the Reset button, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app.

### 5. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not sell, rent or share any personal information. Apart from the advertising data that Google collects as described in section 3, no data is shared with third parties. Information may be disclosed only if required by law.

### 6. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions needed for advertising: Internet and network state access, and the advertising ID permission (plus related permissions added automatically by Google's ads SDK). The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

### 7. Data retention and security

Local game data is kept on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. The developer uses commercially reasonable means to protect the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

### 8. Children

The app is not directed at children under the age of 16, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the developer and it will be addressed promptly.

### 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU/EEA or the UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Because the developer does not hold personal data, requests about advertising data should be made to Google using the tools listed above; you can also contact the developer for help.

### 10. Links to other sites

Ads and links in the app (for example, to Google Play) may lead to third-party websites. The developer is not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

### 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

### 12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: [bohdan.shpanchuk@gmail.com](mailto:bohdan.shpanchuk@gmail.com)
